# Code of conduct

## Our promise

We want Catchbox to be a friendly place to work on, whoever you are. We promise to treat everyone with respect, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, looks, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and say sorry when you get something wrong.
- Focus on what's best for the people who use Catchbox.

## What's not okay

- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or unwanted attention.
- Sharing someone's private details without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find out of place at work.

## Reporting a problem

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, email the maintainer, Jawad Ul Hadi, at jawadulhadicc@gmail.com. Every report is read, kept private, and answered quickly.

## What happens next

Maintainers may remove comments, code, or issues that break these rules, and may ban people who keep breaking them — temporarily or for good.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
